# What you can do with Outlay

Outlay is a shared expense tracker for your household. Everyone who lives together
can log what they spend, see where the money goes, and settle up who owes whom —
on the web or installed on your phone.

_Last updated: 2026-06-22_

## Track your spending
- Add an expense in seconds: amount, date, category, who paid, and an optional note.
- Edit or remove any expense; on a phone, swipe a row to delete it.
- Money is shown in your household's currency, with totals that always add up.

## Keep things organized
- **Categories** — group spending (Groceries, Utilities, Transport, Farming, …)
  with their own icon and color; add your own.
- **Members** — the people in your home. Give each a name, optionally an email,
  and an admin or member role. People who don't use the app can still be tracked.

## See where the money goes
A dashboard that updates as you add expenses:
- This month's total, number of expenses, and daily average.
- A daily-spending chart over the last 30 days.
- A breakdown by category and a breakdown by member.
- Your most recent expenses at a glance.

## Settle up (who owes whom)
- See each member's balance — who's owed money and who owes.
- Get the simplest set of payments to square everyone up, with a one-tap
  "Settle up" to record a payment.
- Keep a history of settlements; choose per member who's included in settle-up.

## Activity feed
- A running log of everything that happened in the household — expenses added or
  edited, settlements, members and categories changed, imports — with who did it
  and when, grouped by day.

## More than one home
- Keep separate households (e.g. home and a family farm) and switch between them.
- Each household has its own currency and its own accent color theme.

## Bring data in and out
- **Import** expenses from a CSV file (for example, a Google Form or spreadsheet) —
  Outlay detects the columns, suggests categories, and skips duplicates.
- **Export** your expenses to CSV, Excel, or PDF.

## Signing in and sharing
- Sign in securely with your Google account.
- Invite family members by email; they get access on their next sign-in.
- A private shared-passcode mode is available for the household owner.

## Use it like an app
- Install Outlay to your phone's home screen (it's a Progressive Web App).
- Works offline, with a polished mobile experience and light/dark themes.
